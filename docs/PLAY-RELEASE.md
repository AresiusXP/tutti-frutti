# Google Play release checklist

Manual, step-by-step checklist for publishing the Android app ([`ANDROID.md`](ANDROID.md)) on Google Play.
It is a working checklist, **not legal advice and not a statement of compliance**: Play's requirements change,
so confirm every item against the current Play Console screens and policy pages, and treat the table in section 9
as the decisions that only the owner can make. The website stays as it is and stays reachable everywhere.

Constants used below: package `xyz.aresius.tuttifrutti` (permanent), name **Tutti Frutti** (the same in every
language; *Basta* is not a name, only the call a player makes: "¡BASTA!" in Spanish, "Stop!" in English and Dutch),
website `https://tuttifrutti.aresius.xyz`, audience 13+, first countries **Argentina and the Netherlands**.

## 1. Developer account

- [ ] New **personal** developer account (`aresiusxp@gmail.com`): pay the registration fee and complete
  **identity verification** (legal name, address, ID document); the public developer name and contact details
  are shown on the store listing.
- [ ] **Device verification** with the **Play Console mobile app** on a real Android phone signed in to the
  same account (required for new personal accounts); keep the phone, it is also the first test device.
- [ ] Payments profile completed (even for a free app); two-step verification on the Google account.
- [ ] **Trader status** (EU/UK consumer law; Play Console > Manage account > Account details > About you, then
  the on-screen trader prompt). Google announced the declaration on 29 Oct 2026 (Play policy announcement,
  support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18100766): it **appears in Play Console from November
  2026** (it was not there on 31 Oct 2026 for this personal account) and **every account must complete it by
  1 February 2027**, or updates are blocked and apps can be removed worldwide (answer/17594752). Until then a
  personal account shows its legal name, country and email. **Recommended: declare "not a trader"**. The app is free, has no ads, no purchases and no monetisation inside
  the app, and it is the hobby of one individual. The website (not the app) shows voluntary Cafecito / Ko-fi support
  links, so whether that makes the owner a trader is the **owner's judgement**; if in doubt, declaring "trader"
  publishes the address, phone and email on the listing. Re-check when anything about money changes.
- [ ] Understand the testing requirement for new personal accounts: before applying for production access you
  need a **closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days** (section 6).

## 2. Create the app

- [ ] Play Console > Create app: name **Tutti Frutti**, default language Spanish (Argentina, `es-419`/`es-AR`
  as offered), App (not Store listing only), **Game**, **Free**, accept the declarations.
- [ ] Enrol in **Play App Signing** (default). The first uploaded `.aab` fixes the package name forever.
- [ ] Produce the signed bundle with the release workflow and upload it to **Internal testing**
  ([`ANDROID.md`](ANDROID.md), section 11). Never upload the debug APK.
- [ ] Copy the **app-signing SHA-256** from Test and release > App signing into the website's
  `web/static/.well-known/assetlinks.json` (owned by the website side), deploy, and verify with
  `adb shell pm get-app-links xyz.aresius.tuttifrutti` and the Digital Asset Links API
  ([`ANDROID.md`](ANDROID.md), sections 7-8). Without this the app opens a Custom Tab with a URL bar.

## 3. Store listing (es, en, nl)

One listing per language, all with the constant name **Tutti Frutti**. Check the current character limits in the
console (title 30, short description 80, full description 4000 at the time of writing).

- [ ] Title: `Tutti Frutti` in es, en and nl.
- [ ] Short and full descriptions per language. Describe the game (real-time multiplayer word game, private
  rooms with a 4-character code, public rooms with automated opponents, optional Google sign-in). **Never mention
  support links, tips or donations** (gate G2). Do not call the game "Basta"; in English and Dutch text the call
  is "Stop!" and no Spanish word or character appears. The "SAFE AND FAIR" paragraph is publishable once the
  release with the safety layer is deployed (gate G4).
- [ ] **App icon 512 × 512** PNG (Play applies its own rounded mask, so use a full-bleed square:
  `web/static/icons/maskable-512.png`; `icon-512.png` has rounded transparent corners).
- [x] **Feature graphic 1024 × 500** (PNG, no alpha): `android/store/feature-graphic-1024x500.png`, regenerated by
  `android/scripts/generate-feature-graphic.mjs` (the constant name only, no claims). Listing text per language:
  `android/store/listing/{es,en,nl}.md` (short description ≤ 80 characters, checked).
- [x] **Phone screenshots** (7 per language, 1080 × 1920 PNG, no alpha): `cd e2e && npm run store:screenshots`
  plays one short game per language on the compose stack, launched like the Android app (no support link), and
  writes `android/store/screenshots/{es,en,nl}/01-home … 07-final.png` (not committed: regenerate after UI
  changes). Upload each language's set under that language's listing. The Brand answers (trademarks) are never in
  view. Tablet screenshots are optional and not made.
- [ ] Category, contact email, website `https://tuttifrutti.aresius.xyz`, privacy policy
  `https://tuttifrutti.aresius.xyz/privacy`.

## 4. App content declarations

Answer each one honestly from the shipped behaviour, not from what is convenient. Re-answer when a feature changes.

- [ ] **Privacy policy**: `https://tuttifrutti.aresius.xyz/privacy` (must match what the app does; the web page is
  kept true by its tests).
- [ ] **Ads**: *No, the app contains no ads.* Advertising ID: *No* (the merged manifest has no `AD_ID` permission;
  `audit-manifest.mjs` fails on any new permission).
- [ ] **App access**: all functionality is reachable without signing in (guest); Google sign-in is optional.
  Give the reviewer the instructions of section 8.
- [ ] **Target audience and content**: select the age groups of gate G1 (recommended: **13-15, 16-17 and 18+**,
  see the G1 notes in section 9; the owner confirms the decision when filling in this form). Do **not** select any
  group under 13 and do not present the app as designed for children. Do not enrol in the Families programme.
- [ ] **Content rating (IARC questionnaire)**: answer every question truthfully, including user-generated content
  and user interaction (players see each other's nicknames, avatars, answers and custom category labels, and can
  report them). **Never preselect or copy a rating**: the rating is the output of the questionnaire.
- [ ] **Data safety** (inventory below), including *data is encrypted in transit* and *users can request deletion*.
- [ ] **Account deletion**: web link `https://tuttifrutti.aresius.xyz/delete-account` (the in-app path is "Delete
  my account" in the stats screen; both must work).
- [ ] Government app / financial features / health / news / other special declarations: *No* unless the console
  question genuinely applies.
- [ ] **Countries/regions**: Argentina and the Netherlands only for the first release.

### Data safety inventory

Source of truth: [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md) (section 9b "Privacy", "Privacy policy page", "AI judge") and
`/privacy`. Confirm each category and the collected/shared wording against the console's help text.

| Data | Why | Required or optional | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guest identifier (cookie `tf_guest`, 1 year) and session (`tf_session`, 30 days) | Keep a player's seat and session | Required to play | First-party cookies set by the website, `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, `SameSite=Lax` |
| Nickname and avatar (emoji + colour) | Shown to the other players in a room | Required to play | Chosen by the user; stored in the browser (`tf.nickname`, `tf.avatar`) and in the room while it exists |
| Answers, custom category labels | Gameplay; shown to the room during review | Required to play | A room ends when everyone leaves, after 30 minutes without connected players or two minutes unclaimed; its snapshot is deleted once it was not updated for 6 h, but a live room keeps refreshing it, so active room data can outlive 6 h |
| Terms acceptance, reports of other players, blocks (once shipped) | Moderation and abuse handling | Acceptance required to play; reports and blocks optional | Acceptance: guest id, terms version, time, language, 13+ confirmation. Reports: reporter and target ids, the text shown in the game, reason and comment, kept until 30 days after resolution and at most 90 days. Blocks: until unblocked. Re-check the exact retention in `DESIGN.md` ("Safety") and `/privacy` when each feature ships |
| Google sign-in identifiers (`sub`, display name; scopes `openid profile`, no email, no picture) and stats | Optional account with lifetime statistics | Optional | Kept until the account is deleted (in-app or `/delete-account`) |
| AI judge: the category label, the letter, the language and the non-empty answers of **all players** that start with the letter (never nicknames or ids) sent to **Cloudflare Workers AI** | Advisory verdicts when a signed-in host asks | Optional | Cloudflare acts as a processor and does not train on the data; the label and answers are typed by players; declare it as the console requires for service providers |
| Server logs with player ids | Operation and abuse protection | Required | Access logs (method, path, status, duration; request headers dropped) and WebSocket events (player id). Kept **7 days** (Fluent Bit to Loki, `retention_period` 168h). **No player IP addresses reach the servers**: Cloudflare Tunnel is in front and the servers only see internal addresses; Cloudflare processes the IP addresses as network provider (declare it as the console requires for service providers) |
| Backups | | | **None**: the database and its volume are not backed up, so deleted data (an account, a purged report) is gone at once and is in no backup |
| Transit | | | Everything over HTTPS/WSS |

The Android shell itself collects nothing, stores nothing (backup disabled), has no permissions and includes no
analytics, ads, Firebase, push or billing SDK.

## 5. Reviewer access instructions (paste into App access)

> No account is needed. Open the app and choose a nickname and an avatar: you are a guest.
> **Public game with automated opponents:** from the home screen open the public rooms list and join a room, or
> create a public room: automated opponents fill the table so a single reviewer can play a full round.
> **Private game:** create a private room, then join it with the 4-character code (or the invite link / QR) from a
> second device or a second browser profile; both players can play a whole round and the review phase.
> **Safety features:** during the review phase use the report and block actions on another player's answer or
> name; in the stats screen (Google sign-in, optional) use "Delete my account"; the web deletion page is
> `https://tuttifrutti.aresius.xyz/delete-account`.
> There are no personal Google credentials, no hidden bypass, no test accounts and no features behind a paywall;
> Google sign-in is optional and every feature other than lifetime statistics works as a guest.

Update this text when the report/block features are in the shipped build; do not describe a screen that does not
exist yet.

## 6. Test tracks

- [ ] **Internal testing** (up to 100 testers, no review wait): install from Play on at least two real devices and
  run the manual matrix of [`ANDROID-PLAN.md`](ANDROID-PLAN.md) (verified links cold/warm, lowercase and expired/full
  rooms, Google login/cancel/logout/deletion, process death, offline first launch, Back/keyboard/modals, font scaling,
  TalkBack, cutouts, gesture/3-button navigation, screen lock, battery saver, Wi-Fi/mobile switches).
- [ ] **Closed testing**: recruit genuine testers, make sure **at least 12 stay opted in for 14 continuous days**,
  and collect real feedback. Do not buy or fake testers. The plan:
  - **Internal testers do not count.** A tester who is on the internal track must **leave it** (opt out of
    internal testing) before their closed-test days count.
  - **Countries:** on the default closed track, Countries/regions > **Unsynced** from production, then select
    **all countries**, so testers anywhere can install it. Production stays Argentina and the Netherlands.
  - **Testers through Google Groups:** the owner's own group (friends and family, real people from Argentina and the
    Netherlands first) and, optionally, AppHive's group `apphive-testers@googlegroups.com`. Aim for **15-20** so
    drop-outs do not break the minimum: an opt-out restarts **that tester's** 14 days.
  - **Ask for real use:** one public round a day (public rooms fill with automated opponents, so one tester can play
    alone) and a private-room session with several testers once a week.
  - **Ship 1-2 updates** during the test (a website change reaches the testers by itself; a shell change needs a new
    closed-track release) and read **Testing feedback** (Monitor and improve > Ratings and reviews > Testing
    feedback).
  - **AppHive** (only as a supplement): the free test-for-test exchange requires opening 16 other apps every day, or
    you are removed and your testers are told to uninstall; the paid "12+ testers for $24.99" option has no refund
    and no approval guarantee: **not recommended**. **Never use AppHive's "Review Task"**: points for Play reviews are
    incentivised ratings, prohibited by the [Ratings, Reviews and Installs
    policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898684).
- [ ] Apply for **production access** in Play Console once the 14 days are complete; answer the questionnaire
  about the test and the app honestly: where the testers came from (own group, AppHive), and that the AppHive testers
  are other developers, not players from Argentina or the Netherlands. Review time varies.
- [ ] **Production** release, countries limited to Argentina and the Netherlands; consider a staged rollout.
  Expand countries only after the gates of section 9 are resolved for them.

Sequence: internal → closed (12 testers, 14 days) → production access → production (AR + NL).

## 7. Before every release

- [ ] `ci-android` green on the release commit; `android/version-code.txt` higher than the last upload.
- [ ] Release workflow artifact verified (`sha256sum -c`), signed with the upload key, uploaded by hand.
- [ ] No new permission, no new SDK; if one is added, redo the Data safety form and this checklist.
- [ ] Release notes per language; roll out to internal first, then promote.
- [ ] After rollout: watch Android vitals (crashes, ANRs) and the Play Console policy centre.

## 8. After publishing

- Website changes ship through the website; only shell changes need a new Play release (see
  [`ANDROID.md`](ANDROID.md), section 13).
- Keep `assetlinks.json` valid; never remove the app-signing fingerprint.
- Every year: target-API-level and 16 KB page-size checks ([`ANDROID.md`](ANDROID.md), section 14).

### G1 notes: minors and consent (recommended)

* **Keep 13+** in Argentina and the Netherlands. Play Console target audience: **13-15, 16-17 and 18+**; nothing
  under 13, no Families programme.
* **Legal bases: contract** (running the game the player asked for) and **legitimate interest** (safety, abuse
  protection, logs). Nothing is processed on the basis of consent, so the age-of-consent rules for information
  society services (GDPR Art. 8; in the Netherlands 16 years, UAVG art. 5) are not triggered. The privacy page has
  to name the same bases (owned by the website side).
* **Google accounts** are 16+ in the Netherlands: younger players play as guests or with a supervised account; the
  sign-in stays optional and nothing but lifetime statistics needs it.
* **Residual risk:** Google may treat 13-15 year-olds in the Netherlands as children for its own policies. Fallback:
  one **16+** rule everywhere (age groups 16-17 and 18+, the terms and the age confirmation changed with it).
* Status: **recommended; the owner confirms when filling in the Target audience form.**

### G2 notes: support links (final)

The links stay **hidden inside the Android app** and are **kept on the website**. Reasons:

* Play's [Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738) forbids leading
  users of an app to a payment method other than Play's billing.
* The tip exception ([Payments policy FAQ](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818),
  *direct tips or contributions from user to creator*) needs **100% of the tip to reach the creator** and grants
  nothing digital; in practice it is refused for tips to the app's own developer.
* Neither destination passes 100%: **Ko-fi** takes 0% on the free plan (5% on Standard) plus the payment processor's
  fees; **Cafecito** takes a commission plus Mercado Pago's fees.
* The EEA [external offers programme](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14372887)
  requires a registered business; Argentina has no such programme.
* Both platforms require supporters to be **18+**, while the app's audience starts at 13.
* Enforcement seen in practice (anecdotal): a paypal.me tip button rejected (Play Community thread 438686440, June
  2026); AnkiDroid's Open Collective link rejected (August 2026); StreetComplete flagged in 2022 for a GitHub page
  that showed donation information.
* So the **store listing must not mention support, tips or donations** either (section 3 and
  `android/store/listing/`).
* **Revisit** when Google's billing choice for the rest of the world arrives (announced for 30 September 2027).

### G4 notes: moderation commitments (resolved)

[`MODERATION.md`](MODERATION.md) holds the operator's procedure: reports checked daily (goal) and decided within 7
days, urgent safety cases acted on within 24 hours of being seen (with whom to tell in the Netherlands and
Argentina), notices of illegal content acknowledged in 3 days and decided in 7, appeals in 14 days, data-protection
requests without undue delay and at the latest within one month (Argentina: access within 10 days, correction or
deletion within 5 business days, Ley 25.326 arts. 14 and 16), account deletion by email (answered within one month at
the latest and the account deleted within one month after its owner has shown it is theirs; Argentina: answered within
5 business days and deleted within 5 business days after that), ATKM removal orders, the ntfy alerts
(`TuttiFruttiUrgentReport`, `TuttiFruttiReportWaiting`)
and what happens during an absence (`moderate public disable` turns public games off). The terms page (`/terms`,
section "Our response times"), the privacy policy and the deletion page state the same commitments and must not
promise more. Keep public games **on** while a Play review or a closed test runs: the reviewer instructions above
rely on a public room with automated opponents. The "SAFE AND FAIR" paragraph of
the listing can be published once the release with the safety layer (terms, reports, blocks, `moderate`) is
deployed.
