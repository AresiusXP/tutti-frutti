## Full description (4000)

Tutti Frutti is the classic pen-and-paper word game, played live on your phone.

A random letter appears. Everybody races to fill in one answer per category that starts with it: a name, an animal, a fruit, a country, a city, a colour... The first player with everything filled in calls "Stop!" and the round ends for everyone. Then you all review the answers together.

HOW IT SCORES
- 10 points for a valid answer nobody else wrote.
- 5 points for a valid answer that someone else wrote too.
- 0 points for an empty or rejected answer.
Players can reject an answer in the review, and a signed-in host can ask an optional AI judge for a second opinion on the answers of one category. The AI judge is advisory: the players decide.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Private rooms: create a room, share the 4-character code, a link or a QR code, and play with friends.
- Public rooms: browse open games, or start one and let automated opponents fill the table.
- Pick the categories (classic sets, a surprise mix, or your own), the number of rounds and an optional time limit.
- Play in Spanish, English or Dutch.
- Keep your lifetime statistics by signing in with Google. Signing in is optional: you can always play as a guest.

SAFE AND FAIR
- Players can report other players and their answers, and block players they do not want to play with.
- Reported content is reviewed by a person.

Tutti Frutti needs an internet connection to play. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.

For players aged 13 and over.
